Everyday English

Hudson

Nơi tôi ghi lại những câu tiếng Anh thường dùng hằng ngày để có thể quay lại và dùng khi cần.

Bài viết này sẽ được cập nhật liên tục.

1. Daily report

1.1. Nothing to report

  • I have no major updates today. I’m working on [N], with no blockers at the moment.

1.2. Confirm the expected timeline

  • I should have it ready for QA by the end of this week.
  • I should have it ready for QA in the next two days.

1.3. Waiting for something

  • I’m currently waiting for confirmation from Sirrish before I can continue with these tickets.
  • I’ve reached out to Vlad to review the architecture. Once he’s done, I’ll pick up the production setup immediately.
  • I’m waiting for a response from Vlad on the architecture review. I just wanted to mention it in case anyone can help move it forward.

1.4. Request a meeting or sync with someone

  • I’d like to have a meeting with Anida after the daily call, if that works for you. Otherwise, please suggest a time when we can meet.
  • Anida, can we sync after this call? If that doesn’t work, just let me know a time that’s good for you.
  • I’d like to have a quick sync with Anida after the daily call, if that works for you, Anida. If not, feel free to suggest a time.

1.5. Finished one task, moving on to the next

  • I’m done with the authentication module. I’ll pick up the payment integration today.
  • I’ll be done with the authentication module by the end of this week. Starting next week, I’ll pick up the test automation work. Please let me know if there are other tasks you’d like me to take instead.

1.6. Task delayed by unexpected work

  • I had to work on a login issue yesterday. It came in at the last minute from Sirrish, so I’ll be continuing the payment integration today. I should have it ready for QA by Thursday, one day later than originally planned. Are you okay with that?
